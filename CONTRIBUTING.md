# Contributing

Contributions that improve the lecture script are welcome. Please open an
issue or pull request with enough context for the change to be reviewed.

## Contribution licenses

  [CC BY 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- Code, build scripts, Manim source, Typst theme code, CSS, Nix expressions,
  and workflows are licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you own it or have permission
to contribute it, and you grant the project permission to include, modify,
publish, and redistribute it under the applicable project license above. You
retain your copyright. A copyright transfer is not required by default.

If a contribution mixes content and code, describe the intended boundary in
the pull request. Do not submit material from `resources/books/`,
`resources/content/`, or `solutions/`, and do not submit third-party figures or
text unless their reuse permissions are documented.

## Attribution

Please provide the public name or handle under which you want to be credited
and a short description of your contribution. A pseudonym is fine. The project
does not need private contact details, legal names, or affiliations for the
public contributor list.

Before a contribution is merged, the contributor must agree to the applicable
license and attribution terms. The pull-request template records that
confirmation.
