Appendix: Gaussian CGS translation

The script uses SI throughout; much of the older plasma literature uses Gaussian CGS, and the table below translates the equations and scales used in this script from one system to the other.

Quantity SI Gaussian CGS
Coulomb force 𝐹=𝑞1𝑞2/(4𝜋𝜀0𝑟2) 𝐹=𝑞1𝑞2/𝑟2

Lorentz force 𝑭=𝑞(𝑬+𝒗×𝑩) 𝑭=𝑞(𝑬+𝒗×𝑩/𝑐)
Gauss’s law 𝛁·𝑬=𝜌𝑞/𝜀0 𝛁·𝑬=4𝜋𝜌𝑞

Purely geometric factors, such as the Debye number 𝑁𝐷=(4𝜋/3)𝑛𝜆𝐷3, are identical in both systems. Temperatures quoted as 𝑘𝐵𝑇 in electronvolts carry the same number in both systems; only the conversion to energy differs,
