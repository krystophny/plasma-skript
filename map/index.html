Status of a model or reduction

  1. stated: written down with assumptions and a source
  2. algebra-checked: every identity of the step verified symbolically (SymPy script listed as evidence)
  3. limit-checked: known special cases and limits reproduced symbolically
  4. defect-measured: the reduction error measured by a numerical companion experiment across its regime
  5. literature intake: a cited theorem with its hypotheses
  6. reproduced: the cited proof replayed (checked algebra and complete written proof) and independently reviewed
  7. formally proved: a machine-checked theorem covering the mathematical content of the step
  8. open problem: no rigorous justification is known; the step is formal
  9. erratum: a discrepancy with the cited source

State in the research code kin6d

  • ● implemented: a kin6d case computes the model; the note gives the case and its own evidence label
  • ◐ partial: a reduced slice (for example one space and one velocity dimension) is implemented; the full model is planned
  • ○ planned: on the kin6d roadmap without code
